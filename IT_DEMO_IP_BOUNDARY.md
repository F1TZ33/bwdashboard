# BW Playbook — IT Demo IP Boundary

Prepared for an internal IT demonstration. This document records practical provenance based on the supplied project files; it is not a legal ownership determination.

## Work created and assembled by Hayden Fitzgerald

- BW Playbook concept, information architecture, navigation and dashboard presentation.
- Integration of training, product guidance, resources and operational tools into one internal platform.
- Rack Builder, rack mapping, cabinet design and warehouse mapping workflows.
- Order-capture, QR/label and printable-output workflows.
- Fastener Prefill/template workflow.
- Territory Builder interface and workflow integration. Map facts are obtained from OpenStreetMap/Overpass and are not claimed as original data.
- Cash-up/shift-reconciliation interfaces.
- Product taxonomy, product-page presentation and the brief/detailed guide structure.
- Search interfaces, filters, user interactions and the code used to present permitted reference datasets.
- Sales, service and training content authored specifically for the Playbook, subject to any employment-related ownership arrangements.
- Compilation, cleaning, transformation and organisation work applied to reference material, where that work was performed by Hayden.

## External, manufacturer or company information used

- The legacy Auto X-Ref workbook/dataset. This has been excluded completely from the IT demo package.
- Manufacturer part numbers, dimensions, specifications, nomenclature and interchange facts.
- Timken SBHU and tapered-bearing reference information.
- AMI, Browning, Dodge, FYH, INA, IPTCI, Link-Belt, MRC/SKF, NTN, Sealmaster and other manufacturer interchange information.
- Manufacturer and supplier catalogues, documents, logos, trademarks and product imagery.
- OpenStreetMap/Overpass map and building data.
- BW/Bapcor supplier accounts, contacts, commercial classifications and store-specific supplier choices. Store-based supplier-preference data and functionality have been excluded from this demo.
- Bearing Wholesalers/Bapcor branding and any pre-existing corporate material.

## Mixed or derivative components

For catalogue/search modules, the interface, search logic, normalisation, filters and presentation may be Hayden's implementation, while the underlying part numbers, dimensions and interchange facts come from external catalogues or company reference material. Demonstrating the software does not assert ownership of those underlying facts, trademarks or source publications.

## Excluded from this IT demo

- `test/auto-x-ref/` and its source dataset.
- The supplier-preference dataset and store configuration.
- The supplier-preference management module.
- Store-selection and product-level supplier-preference functionality.
- Search and documentation references that would reveal the excluded Auto X-Ref module or supplier-preference feature.
